SE.Pay is now Stripe. Find out how to set up and manage your tips with Stripe [here](/revenue/stripe). SE.Pay balance can be withdrawn until December 31, 2026. If you didn't withdraw your balance before the deadline, please contact Adyen support for assistance.

## FAQ

**Do I have to move to Stripe?**
Yes, to keep receiving card tips through StreamElements. PayPal tipping is unaffected either way; see [Tipping](/revenue/tipping).

**Are fees changing?**
StreamElements aims to keep fees competitive; exact figures show up during Stripe onboarding.

**Is Stripe available in more countries than SE.Pay?**
Yes — Stripe supports more regions and currencies than SE.Pay.

**Is my information secure?**
Yes. Stripe is a PCI Level 1 certified payment processor with industry-leading security, fraud protection, and identity verification systems.

**If I withdraw my SE.Pay balance on December 31, 2026, what happens next, and when will I receive my funds?**
If your withdrawal was successfully submitted on December 31, 2026, your payment will proceed through the normal withdrawal process. Payments to your bank account can take a few business days to fully process.

**If I withdraw my SE.Pay balance before December 31, 2026, but my withdrawal failed, was reversed or was rejected after SE.Pay closed, what happens to my money?**
Please contact Adyen support for assistance.
