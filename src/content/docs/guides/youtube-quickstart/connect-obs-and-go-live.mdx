Part 4 of the YouTube setup series — the step where everything comes together in OBS. Already streaming? Skip to [5. Set Up Your Chatbot](/guides/youtube-quickstart/set-up-your-chatbot).

## Install SE.Live

SE.Live is StreamElements' OBS plugin — it brings your dashboard, chat, and stream controls right into OBS, so you don't need a second monitor just to run your overlay and check chat.

**Requirements:** OBS Studio 31.x or newer. SE.Live is available for Windows, with a macOS beta.

![The Desktop Apps page in your dashboard, where SE.Live is downloaded from](/img/youtube-quickstart/desktop-apps.jpg)

<Steps>

1. Download the installer from the [SE.Live page](https://streamelements.com/selive) — choose the Mac beta if you're on macOS.
2. Close OBS, then run the installer and choose your install folder.
3. Reopen OBS **as Administrator** and look for the new **StreamElements** menu in the menu bar.

</Steps>

:::note[OBS says it didn't shut down properly?]
If OBS shows a crash-recovery prompt after installing, choose **Run in Normal Mode** — this is routine after installing a plugin, not a sign anything went wrong.
:::

## Connect your accounts

The first time SE.Live opens in OBS, it asks you to connect a channel.

![The Connect your channel screen in OBS, with Twitch, YouTube, X, and Kick options](/img/youtube-quickstart/connect-channel.webp)

<Steps>

1. Click **YOUTUBE** and follow the prompts — the same Google sign-in flow you used when creating your StreamElements account.
2. On the **I'd like to start with...** screen, choose **New setup** (choose **Multi-streaming Setup** instead if you're streaming to more than one platform at once — see [Set up multistreaming](/selive/guides/multistreaming)).

</Steps>

![The Welcome to SE.Live screen, choosing between Multi-streaming Setup and New setup](/img/youtube-quickstart/setup-wizard-start.png)

## Run the setup wizard

New setup walks you through a short wizard that configures your camera, microphone, and capture source automatically — no need to add them by hand in the **Sources** panel.

![The Camera and microphone step of the SE.Live Setup Wizard](/img/youtube-quickstart/wizard-camera-mic.png)

![The Display capture step of the SE.Live Setup Wizard](/img/youtube-quickstart/wizard-display-capture.png)

<Steps>

1. **Camera & microphone.** Pick your microphone and camera (or leave camera as **No camera** if you're not showing your face).
2. **Display capture.** Choose which display to stream — you can switch this to a game capture later.
3. Click **Finish setup**.

</Steps>

![The Woo-hoo! finish step of the SE.Live Setup Wizard, with a Finish setup button](/img/youtube-quickstart/wizard-finish.png)

## Add your YouTube stream key

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings → Stream**, choose **YouTube / YouTube Gaming**.
2. Click **Get stream key** (or find it under [youtube.com/live_dashboard](https://www.youtube.com/live_dashboard) → **Encoder setup**) and paste it in.

</Steps>

:::caution[Keep your stream key private]
Never share it or show it on stream — anyone with it can stream to your channel.
:::

## Add your overlay

<Steps>

1. Copy the URL of the overlay you built in the [previous step](/guides/youtube-quickstart/build-your-overlay) — from **My Overlays**, open its menu and select **Copy URL**.
2. In OBS Sources, click **+** → **Browser**, name it, and paste the URL.
3. Set **Width** to `1920` and **Height** to `1080` (or your overlay's resolution), and check **Shutdown when source not visible**.

</Steps>

## Keep track of your stream from inside OBS

Open **StreamElements → SE.Live Docks** to add these as dockable panels in OBS — everything you need to run your stream lives right next to your scenes, so you don't need a companion app, a second monitor, or a browser tab open on the side.

![The StreamElements Canvas dock, showing the vertical canvas preview and scene list](/img/youtube-quickstart/canvas-dock.png)

**Canvas** shows a live preview of your scene and lets you switch scenes without leaving OBS — useful if you're running a separate [vertical canvas](/selive/guides/multistreaming) for Shorts or a mobile-friendly layout.

![The YouTube Chat dock before going live](/img/youtube-quickstart/youtube-chat-dock.png)

**YouTube Chat** shows your live chat once you go live, with a shortcut to Live Video Settings and the full Live Dashboard.

![The StreamElements Multi-Streaming dock, with two YouTube destinations toggled on](/img/youtube-quickstart/multistreaming-dock.png)

**Multi-Streaming** lists every destination you've connected and lets you toggle each on or off before you go live — only relevant if you chose Multi-streaming Setup earlier.

![The Activity Feed dock before any events have come in](/img/youtube-quickstart/activity-feed-dock.png)

**Activity Feed** mirrors the [Activity Feed](/guides/youtube-quickstart/dashboard-tour) from your dashboard — Super Chats, members, and subscribers appear here in real time.

Dock any combination you like around the OBS window — a typical layout keeps Activity Feed and chat on one side and the canvas preview on the other:

![OBS with the Activity Feed, Canvas, Multi-Streaming, and YouTube Chat docks arranged around the main preview](/img/youtube-quickstart/obs-docks-layout.webp)

## Go live

<Steps>

1. Double-check your title, category, and privacy setting are correct for this stream.
2. Click **Start Streaming**.

</Steps>

That's it — you're live on YouTube. One thing left to do: get the chatbot actually responding in your chat, which is the last step in this series.
