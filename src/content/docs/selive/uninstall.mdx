## Back up your profile first (optional)

If you might reinstall later, save your OBS profile before uninstalling:

<Steps>

1. Open File Explorer and go to `%appdata%\obs-studio\basic`.
2. Copy both folders inside it to another location.

</Steps>

After reinstalling, paste the folders back into that same directory to restore your setup.

## Uninstall

<Steps>

1. Open the **Start Menu** and search for **Control Panel**.
2. Open **Programs and Features** (Large Icons view) or **Uninstall a Program** (Category view, under Programs).
3. Find **StreamElements SE.Live** in the list and select it.
4. Click **Uninstall** and follow the prompts.

</Steps>

Rebooting your PC afterward is recommended.

### If uninstalling fails

Install OBS over your current installation folder, then try uninstalling SE.Live again. If it still fails, reinstall OBS from the [latest version](https://obsproject.com/download) and retry.
